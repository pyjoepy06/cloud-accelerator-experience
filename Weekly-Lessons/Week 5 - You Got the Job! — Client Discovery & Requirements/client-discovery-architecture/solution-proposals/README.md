# Solution Proposals

[Back to Week 3](../../README.md)

## Goal

Explain a migration recommendation in terms a client can evaluate.

## One-page proposal structure

1. Business problem and measurable success criteria.
2. Scope and inventory evidence.
3. Recovery requirements and application dependencies.
4. Proposed 6 R strategy by application, with alternatives and rationale.
5. Preliminary architecture and migration waves.
6. Timeline, validation, rollback, and acceptance owners.
7. One-time and recurring cost categories, assumptions, and contingency.
8. Risks, unanswered questions, and approvals needed.

Avoid a firm quote or promised migration date when requirements are unknown. Separate confirmed facts, estimates, and assumptions.

**Success:** A reader can see why the approach fits the business, what evidence supports it, and which decisions remain open.
